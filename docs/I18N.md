## Rules

1. **English is the canonical source.** Facts, dates and entry selection are decided in the English files and in `data/events.json`. Translations never introduce facts that are not in the canonical source.
2. **Same-day sync:** en + zh-CN are updated together for every daily entry. zh-TW is converted from zh-CN (Taiwan vocabulary) and checked. ja and ko sync within the same update whenever possible.
3. **Lagging languages stay honest:** es, fr, de, pt-BR and ru currently have full READMEs and localized TIMELINE frames. Until their timelines are fully translated, those files say so plainly and link to the canonical English timeline. Never present a partial translation as complete.
4. **Dates and numbers are language-neutral:** use ISO dates (YYYY-MM-DD) everywhere; never localize a date into an ambiguous format.
5. **Proper nouns keep their original names** (model names, product names, people), with a local-language gloss on first mention where helpful.
6. **The "good it brings" column is translated for meaning, not word by word** — the point is what the event means for ordinary people in that culture.
7. Daily drafts are reviewed in Chinese by the maintainer, but the published master is English first, per rule 2.

## Adding a language

Create `i18n/<locale>/README.md` and `TIMELINE.md`, translate the README fully, add the language to every switcher line and to the status table in the root README, and note it here.
