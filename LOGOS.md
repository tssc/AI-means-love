# Logo Evolution · Logo 进化史

**EN** · [ZH-CN](i18n/zh-CN/LOGOS.md)

A logo is a family face: it changes as the company grows up. Here are the AI era's most recognizable marks, and what each redesign was really saying. *(The marks belong to their owners; this page tells their stories and links to official sources — it does not host logo files.)*

## OpenAI — the knot that learned to soften

| When | The mark | What changed |
|---|---|---|
| 2015 | Wordmark only | A research lab's plain signature; no symbol yet |
| 2017–18 | The "blossom" knot appears | A geometric knot of interlocking shapes — intelligence as something woven |
| Feb 2025 | Blossom redrawn + OpenAI Sans | First full rebrand: larger center, uniform line thickness, a bespoke typeface whose "O" is round outside and deliberately imperfect inside — "more organic and more human" |

The story in one line: a math knot, re-drawn by humans to look *less* robotic — with AI tools used only to calculate type weights. Sources: [Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/91273217/open-ai-rebrand-chat-gpt-logo), [The Telegraph](https://www.telegraphindia.com/science-tech/openai-undergoes-its-first-ever-rebrand/cid/2082138).

## Google DeepMind — two labs, one spark

DeepMind (founded 2010, acquired by Google in 2014) and Google Brain ran as two separate families for a decade. In April 2023 they merged into **Google DeepMind**, and the Gemini era brought a four-pointed **spark** — a star that is also a glint of light. The story: rivals under one roof, rebranded as a single spark.

## Anthropic / Claude — the humanist star

Anthropic (2021) chose a warm, hand-drawn-feeling starburst and an unusually warm palette for Claude. In a field of cold geometry, the mark deliberately feels like a sun drawn by a person. The story: safety research with a human face.

## Meta AI — the ring that moved into your chats

Meta's AI assistant era (announced 2023, rolled out across WhatsApp, Instagram and Messenger in 2024) arrived with a blue-to-purple gradient **loop** — an open ring, halfway between a letter "a" and an orbit. The story: AI not as a destination app, but a ring around the conversations you already have. In 2026 the family grew again with the Muse personal agent.

## DeepSeek — the whale from the deep

DeepSeek's blue whale became one of the most seen marks of January 2025, when R1 topped app charts worldwide. A whale is a good emblem for the lab's style: huge, quiet, and from the deep — surfacing with open weights. The story: the deep sea, open-sourced.

## Mistral — pixel blocks in the wind

Mistral AI (2023, Paris) flies horizontal pixel stripes in orange-red-yellow — part flag, part retro game sprite, named after the cold Mistral wind of southern France. The story: European, fast, and a little playful — efficiency you can see at sixteen pixels.
