## How to read it

Every model you have ever used has ancestors. This tree reads left to right:

- **The trunk (grey)** — the old ideas: Turing's test (1950), the perceptron (1957), backpropagation (1986), the deep-learning revival (2006), AlexNet (2012) and AlphaGo (2016).
- **The big bang (red)** — one 2017 paper, *Attention Is All You Need*, introduced the **Transformer**. Almost everything alive today descends from it.
- **The five families (colors)** — the Transformer's descendants, each with its own temperament.

Every node on this tree is an entry in [TIMELINE.md](TIMELINE.md), with dates and sources.

## The five families

**🟠 The GPT Family — the famous firstborn.**
GPT-1 (2018) was a modest research model. Its grandchild ChatGPT (2022) introduced AI to a hundred million people in two months, and GPT-4 / GPT-4o turned the family into the world's default assistant.

**🟢 The Open Family — the generous branch.**
TensorFlow (2015) gave away the tools; LLaMA (2023) gave away the model itself. DeepSeek-R1 (2025) proved open weights could reason at the frontier, and Muse Spark Open (2026) kept the tradition: the family's motto is *take it, run it, it's yours*.

**🩷 The China Family — the fast runners.**
ERNIE and Qwen arrived in 2023, weeks after GPT-4. By 2025–26 the branch was setting its own records: DeepSeek's efficiency, Kimi K3.1 Pro's 2.8-trillion-parameter open flagship, and Baidu's Unlimited OCR 2 reading whole archives in one pass.

**🟣 The Creative Family — the artists.**
GANs (2014) taught machines to imagine; DALL·E and CLIP (2021) connected words to pictures; Stable Diffusion (2022) put image generation on home computers; Sora (2024) extended the imagination to video.

**🔵 Agents & Science — the branch that does things.**
AlphaFold 2 (2020) folded proteins and helped AI toward two Nobel Prizes (2024). Copilot (2021) moved into the editor. By 2025 agents were doing multi-step work, and in 2026 personal agents like Muse arrived — models that don't just answer, but act.

## One sentence to remember

> The trunk is mathematics, the big bang is attention, and the branches are choices: closed or open, chat or create, answer or act.
