## 发生了什么

百度文心团队 11 月 5 日发布 ERNIE-5.5-Flash-Base，在 Hugging Face 与 ModelScope 开放下载权重。这是一个纯文本稀疏 MoE 基础模型：总参数 2800 亿，每 token 仅激活 140 亿，用 33.5 万亿 token 从零预训练（Muon 优化器、FP8 混合精度），43 层（1 层 dense + 42 层 Multi-Router Latent MoE），每层 512 个路由专家取 top-10 外加 1 个共享专家；37 层用重度压缩注意力（每 128 个历史 token 压缩为一条缓存），6 层保留 token 级精细检索；训练上下文 128K，微调与推理支持 100 万 token；提供 FP8 与 BF16 两种 safetensors 权重，支持 SGLang 与 vLLM 部署。

关键在「Base」二字：这是预训练结束时的检查点，尚未经过中训练与后训练，没有对话对齐、助手行为或拒答策略，定位是供开发者做领域微调、指令训练与强化学习研究的共同起点，也是对照评测训练方法的固定基线。百度表示，经过后训练的 ERNIE 5.5 Flash 将随后发布。自 2025 年 6 月文心 4.5 系列之后，这是百度时隔约 16 个月再次开源千亿参数级大模型——其间发布的文心 5.0、5.1 均未开源。

官方统一评测（与 DeepSeek-V4-Flash-Base、腾讯 Hy3-preview-Base、小米 MiMo-V2.5-Base 同管线、同提示、同少样本设置）：ERNIE 在多数基准领先同类 Base 模型，如 MMLU 93.3、HumanEval+ 82.3；但并非全胜——FullStackBench 上 Hy3-preview 领先（56.7 对 54.6），InfiniteBench 上 DeepSeek-V4-Flash 领先（48.0 对 46.5）。以上均为百度自测数字，未经独立复现。

## 为什么值得被历史记住

这是 2026 年 11 月中国大模型「开放周」的第三块拼图：11 月 4 日腾讯 Hy4 正式版（权重待放）、月之暗面 Kimi K3.1 Pro 权重落地之后，百度以一个 2800 亿参数的纯预训练底座加入——三家走的是三条不同的开放路线：Hy4 先讲防护再放权重、Kimi 兑现日期承诺全量开源、百度则把最贵的预训练一步替研究者做完，只留微调与对齐给下游。它把「效率」做进了架构本身：140 亿激活参数撑起 2800 亿总参数、百万 token 上下文靠压缩注意力控制成本，呼应了智能体时代「算力要花在刀刃上」的共识。

但本条真正的历史注脚在许可证：自定义的 ERNIE 5.5 Flash Base 许可证 v1.0 把地域排除在外（欧盟、英国、韩国不在授权地域内），相关产品或服务过去连续 12 个月营收超 5000 万美元的商用须另获百度书面授权，5000 万美元及以下须一次性报备并显著标注「Powered by ERNIE」，且明文禁止用模型材料及输出去改进无关的 AI 系统。上一代文心 4.5 用的是 Apache 2.0——从完全开放到「有条件开放」，是中国大厂开源路线的第一次明确收紧，值得与模型本身同等记录。

## 它带来的善

对研究者与小团队而言，一个 2800 亿参数级的预训练底座变成可下载的文件：不必重复 33.5 万亿 token 的预训练，就能站在同一条起跑线上做领域微调、做中文与多语种的专门能力、做训练方法的对照实验——能力下放的同时，训练成本的门槛被百度先扛了一段。但须保留提醒：这是 Base 模型而非成品助手，普通用户无法直接对话使用；且许可证的地域与商用门槛意味着，欧盟、英国、韩国的开发者与年营收超 5000 万美元的商业用户不在本次开放的受益范围内。

## English version (canonical)

# 2026-11-05 · Baidu open-sources ERNIE 5.5 Flash Base: a 280B-parameter raw pre-training checkpoint

- **Date**: 2026-11-05
- **Category**: Open source
- **Protagonist**: Baidu ERNIE Team

## What happened

On November 5, Baidu's ERNIE Team released ERNIE-5.5-Flash-Base, publishing downloadable weights on Hugging Face and ModelScope. It is a text-only sparse mixture-of-experts base model: 280 billion total parameters with 14 billion activated per token, pre-trained from scratch on 33.5 trillion tokens (Muon optimizer, FP8 mixed precision), 43 layers (1 dense + 42 Multi-Router Latent MoE), 512 routed experts per MoE layer with top-10 routing plus one shared expert; 37 layers use Heavily Compressed Attention (one cache entry per 128 tokens) and 6 layers keep token-level retrieval; training context 128K, fine-tuning and inference support a 1M-token context; FP8 and BF16 safetensors weights, served with SGLang and vLLM.

The word "Base" is the point: this is the checkpoint at the end of pre-training, before any mid- or post-training, with no chat alignment, assistant behaviour or refusal policy — a common starting point for domain fine-tuning, instruction tuning and reinforcement-learning research, and a fixed baseline for controlled comparisons of training methods. Baidu says a post-trained ERNIE 5.5 Flash will follow. It is Baidu's first open release of a hundred-billion-parameter-class model in about 16 months since the ERNIE 4.5 series of June 2025 — the ERNIE 5.0 and 5.1 models released in between were not open-sourced.

Baidu's unified evaluation (same pipeline, prompts and few-shot settings against DeepSeek-V4-Flash-Base, Tencent Hy3-preview-Base and Xiaomi MiMo-V2.5-Base) puts ERNIE ahead of its fellow base checkpoints on most benchmarks — MMLU 93.3, HumanEval+ 82.3 — but not all: Hy3-preview leads on FullStackBench (56.7 vs 54.6) and DeepSeek-V4-Flash leads on InfiniteBench (48.0 vs 46.5). All company figures, not independently reproduced.

## Why it matters

This is the third piece of China's open-model week in November 2026: after Tencent's Hy4 full release on November 4 (weights still pending) and Moonshot's Kimi K3.1 Pro weights the same day, Baidu joins with a 280B-parameter raw pre-training checkpoint — three different routes to openness: Hy4 explains safeguards before releasing weights, Kimi keeps a dated promise with a full release, and Baidu absorbs the most expensive step, pre-training, leaving fine-tuning and alignment to everyone else. Efficiency is built into the architecture itself: 14B activated parameters carry a 280B model, and a million-token context is held affordable by compressed attention — the agent era's consensus that compute should be spent where it counts.

But this entry's real historical footnote is the licence. The custom ERNIE 5.5 Flash Base Model License Agreement v1.0 excludes territory — the European Union, the United Kingdom and South Korea are outside the licensed territory — requires Baidu's prior written authorization for commercial use once related products or services exceed $50M in annual revenue (at or below that line, a one-time notice and prominent "Powered by ERNIE" attribution), and expressly bars using the licensed materials or their outputs to improve an unrelated AI system. The previous generation, ERNIE 4.5, shipped under Apache 2.0. The move from fully open to conditionally open is the first explicit tightening of a major Chinese lab's open-source terms, and deserves to be recorded alongside the model itself.

## The good it brings

For researchers and small teams, a 280B-class pre-training checkpoint becomes a downloadable file: no one has to repeat a 33.5-trillion-token pre-training run to start from the same line — domain fine-tuning, specialist capability in Chinese and other languages, and controlled experiments on training methods all begin from a shared point, with Baidu having carried the first, most expensive stretch of the cost. Two caveats stay attached: this is a base model, not a finished assistant, so ordinary users cannot chat with it directly; and the licence's territorial and commercial gates mean developers in the EU, UK and South Korea, and commercial users above the $50M line, are outside this release's beneficiaries.
