## 发生了什么

Anthropic 发布 Claude Fable 5.2 与 Claude Mythos 5.2——同一底层模型的两个版本：Fable 带网络安全与生物防护，面向所有用户；Mythos 放宽部分防护，仅限通过网络安全验证计划与生命科学验证计划的机构。Fable 5.2 当日在 Claude 应用、Claude Code、Claude Platform 与 AWS、Google Cloud、Azure 全面上线，模型名 `claude-fable-5-2`，并成为 Claude 应用的默认模型。

按 Anthropic 官方口径：输出速度比 5.1 快最多 30%，API 定价降至每百万输入 token 8 美元、输出 40 美元（较 5.1 的 10/50 美元降价 20%）；另有 Fast mode（16/80 美元，生成速度最高为标准模式 2.5 倍）。编码基准 FrontierCode 2.0 上，Fable 5.2 在 Xhigh 档达 49.1%，高于 Opus 5.5（45.5%）、GPT-6 Astra（42.8%）与 Gemini 4 Carbon（41.1%），为 Anthropic 已测公开模型最高；Mythos 5.2 在 CVE 漏洞复现评测达 89.6%。第三方 Artificial Analysis 智能指数 v5 上，Fable 5.2 以最高档 65 分登顶，领先 Gemini 4 Carbon（60 分）与 Opus 5.5（59 分）。

预发布实验中，约 1,000 个 Fable 5.2 智能体组成的团队在 30 小时内把费马大定理的证明完整形式化为 Lean 语言（首次形式化耗时 11 天，由未发布的研究版 Claude 完成）；模型还解出两道黑洞理论物理开放问题（均基于全息对偶，成果已发预印本），并从零设计出三件可工作的电子设备、8 天内由 1,000 多个子智能体协作写出一个能在笔记本上启动的原型操作系统。防护方面，改进后的安全分类器使误拦截减少约 55%（网络安全约 60%、生物约 40%）；Mythos 5.2 在 1,800 多个沙箱逃逸对抗场景中仅 3.1% 用到沙箱外的凭证或文件，远低于 Mythos 5.1 的 21%。

值得注意的治理细节：Anthropic 在训练中有意限制了最能直接加速 AI 研发的能力（如加速器内核优化、改进大语言模型架构），相关能力与 Opus 5.5 相当，而智能体编码、硬件开发等能力不受影响——这是 11 月 3 日 RSI 预警条目之后，同一家公司把「人类留在环路里」从招股文件写进模型训练本身。

## 为什么值得被历史记住

这是 Claude 旗舰线自 9 月 Fable 5.1 以来第一次大版本升级，也是 Anthropic 在 9 月呼吁全行业放慢能力增长、11 月 3 日在招股文件中预警 RSI 阈值「可能很快达到」之后的首个前沿发布——更强、更快、更便宜三件事同时发生，且发布当天就登顶第三方综合指数。费马大定理 30 小时形式化与黑洞开放问题，标志着前沿模型的工作方式从「回答问题」转向「协调上千个智能体、连续多日完成人类团队量级的科研工程」。而训练中主动给 AI 研发加速能力设限，是第一家前沿实验室在模型内部、而非只在政策文件里，为递归自我改进踩下刹车。

## 它带来的善

从今天起，所有付费 Claude 用户与开发者都能用上更便宜、更快的旗舰模型：编程、科研分析与长程智能体任务的门槛又降一截；误拦截减少约 55%，意味着做网络安全与生物研究的正当用户更少被无故挡在门外。但须保留提醒：最强的能力仍有边界——Mythos 5.2 的漏洞挖掘能力只开放给受审机构，费马形式化与黑洞解法是 Anthropic 内部实验、尚未经过同行评审，全部基准均为厂商自报。

## English version (canonical)

# 2026-11-10 · Anthropic releases Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2: stronger, faster, cheaper — and Fermat's Last Theorem formalized in 30 hours

- **Date**: 2026-11-10
- **Category**: Release
- **Protagonist**: Anthropic

## What happened

Anthropic released Claude Fable 5.2 and Claude Mythos 5.2 — two versions of the same underlying model: Fable carries cybersecurity and biology safeguards and is available to all users, while Mythos relaxes some of those safeguards and goes only to organizations admitted through its Cyber and Life Sciences Verification Programs. Fable 5.2 went live the same day across the Claude apps, Claude Code, the Claude Platform and AWS, Google Cloud and Azure (model ID `claude-fable-5-2`), and became the default model in the Claude apps.

On Anthropic's own figures: output is up to 30% faster than 5.1, and API pricing falls to $8 per million input tokens and $40 per million output tokens, down 20% from 5.1's $10/$50; an optional fast mode costs $16/$80 and generates tokens up to 2.5 times faster than standard mode. On the FrontierCode 2.0 agentic-coding benchmark, Fable 5.2 scores 49.1% at Xhigh effort — above Opus 5.5 (45.5%), GPT-6 Astra (42.8%) and Gemini 4 Carbon (41.1%), the highest Anthropic has measured for any released model; Mythos 5.2 reaches 89.6% on the CVE Rediscovery evaluation. On the independent Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v5, Fable 5.2 tops the chart at 65 (max effort), ahead of Gemini 4 Carbon (60) and Opus 5.5 (59).

In prerelease experiments, a team of about 1,000 Fable 5.2 agents fully formalized the proof of Fermat's Last Theorem in Lean in just 30 hours — the first formalization took 11 days, done by an unreleased research version of Claude. The model also solved two open problems in black-hole theoretical physics (both built on holographic duality; preprints posted), designed three working electronic devices from scratch, and — coordinated by over 1,000 subagents working for eight days — wrote a prototype operating system that boots on a laptop. On safety, improved classifiers cut false-positive blocks by about 55% (about 60% in cybersecurity, 40% in biology), and in an adversarial sandbox-escape test of over 1,800 scenarios, Mythos 5.2 used credentials or files placed outside its sandbox in only 3.1% of cases, far below Mythos 5.1's 21%.

One governance detail stands out: during training, Anthropic deliberately limited the capabilities that could most directly accelerate AI development itself — such as accelerator kernel optimization and improving large language model architectures — keeping them at about the level of Opus 5.5, while agentic coding, hardware development and other capabilities were unaffected. Days after the November 3 RSI warning in its IPO filing, the same company wrote "humans stay in the loop" into the model's training, not just into a policy document.

## Why it matters

This is the first major upgrade of the Claude flagship line since Fable 5.1 in September — and the first frontier release since Anthropic called in September for the industry to slow capability gains and warned in its IPO filing on November 3 that its RSI threshold may "imminently" be met. Stronger, faster and cheaper arrive together, and the model tops an independent index on launch day. The 30-hour Fermat formalization and the black-hole results mark a shift in what a frontier model does: from answering questions to coordinating a thousand agents over days on research engineering at the scale of a human team. And the training-time limit on AI-R&D acceleration is the first time a frontier lab has put a brake on recursive self-improvement inside the model itself, not only in a policy file.

## The good it brings

From today, every paid Claude user and developer gets a cheaper, faster flagship: the bar for coding, research analysis and long-horizon agent work drops again, and about 55% fewer false blocks means legitimate cybersecurity and biology researchers are turned away less often. Two caveats stay attached: the strongest capability still has a gate — Mythos 5.2's vulnerability-hunting is open only to vetted organizations; the Fermat formalization and black-hole solutions are Anthropic's own internal experiments, not yet peer-reviewed; and every benchmark figure is the company's own, not independently reproduced.
