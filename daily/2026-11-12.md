## 发生了什么

Google DeepMind 发布 AlphaGenome 2——新一代 DNA 序列模型，预测 DNA 变异如何影响特定人类细胞类型与细胞状态，而不再是宽泛的组织平均值。模型在 68 种组织的 1.07 亿多个单细胞图谱上训练，采用条件式架构：输入百万碱基 DNA 序列的同时输入目标生物学条件（组织、细胞类型或状态），从而学会同一段 DNA 在不同细胞中被不同解读。在 28 种主要免疫细胞的单细胞图谱 TenK10K 上，它识别出的因果变异为 30%，而上一代 AlphaGenome 微调版为 16%；基因效应方向判断正确率为 43%，上一代为 23%。模型还新增 DNA 甲基化预测，并能对从未见过的细胞状态做预测——无需重新训练，只需几十个细胞的单细胞 RNA 测序快照即可。作为验证，它在计算上复现了一项炎症性肠病（IBD）研究的发现：一个远端调控开关上的变异通过过度激活肠道巨噬细胞中的 ETS2 基因致病，模型准确找出了该因果变异及其作用的细胞类型。模型即日起通过 AlphaGenome API 面向非商业研究开放，商业研究为私人预览。

## 为什么值得被历史记住

这是本时间线第一条基因组学记录：AI 从预测蛋白质结构（AlphaFold）走到了预测 DNA 变异在具体细胞里的作用。过去，基因组关联研究能指出与疾病相关的 DNA 大致区域，却常常找不到真正的因果变异、目标基因和起作用的细胞；AlphaGenome 2 把这三者一次对上号——变异、基因、细胞类型。更重要的是方法上的转折：模型不再把组织当成均值，而是按细胞类型逐个条件预测；连未见过的疾病或疫苗接种后的细胞状态也能处理（官方例子：新冠 mRNA 疫苗研究中，模型区分了激活态与静息态 T 细胞，并发现一个与重症新冠相关的变异在激活态细胞中破坏力更强）。从组织平均到细胞精度，这是基因组 AI 的一代跨越。

## 它带来的善

疾病机制的解释第一次能精确到细胞：研究者可以更快找到致病变异、锁定药物该作用的细胞类型，而不是在整片组织里大海捞针；罕见病与复杂性状的研究门槛也随之降低。但须保留提醒：模型仅供研究使用，未获临床或诊断验证，普通人还用不上；全部性能数字均为 DeepMind 自报，技术论文为预印本、未经同行评审。

## English version (canonical)

# 2026-11-11 · Google DeepMind releases AlphaGenome 2: DNA variants read cell by cell

- **Date**: 2026-11-11
- **Category**: Release
- **Protagonist**: Google DeepMind

## What happened

Google DeepMind released AlphaGenome 2, a next-generation DNA sequence model that predicts how DNA variants affect specific human cell types and cell states, instead of broad tissue averages. Trained on more than 107 million individual cell profiles across 68 tissues, it uses a conditional architecture: alongside a million-letter DNA sequence it takes the target biological condition — a tissue, cell type or state — as input at every stage, learning how the same stretch of DNA is read differently across cells. On TenK10K, a single-cell map of 28 primary immune cell types, it identified 30% of causal variants against 16% for the original AlphaGenome fine-tuned on single-cell data, and called the direction of gene effects correctly 43% of the time against 23%. The model adds DNA methylation as a predicted readout and can predict for cell states it has never seen without retraining, from a single-cell RNA-seq snapshot of just a few dozen cells. As a check, it reproduced a landmark inflammatory bowel disease (IBD) finding computationally: a variant in a distant regulatory switch drives disease by overactivating the ETS2 gene in gut macrophages, and the model singled out the causal variant and the cell type it acts in. AlphaGenome 2 is available from today for non-commercial research via the AlphaGenome API, with commercial research in private preview.

## Why it matters

This is the chronicle's first genomics entry: AI moves from predicting protein structures (AlphaFold) to predicting what a DNA variant does inside a specific cell. Genome-wide association studies could point to broad stretches of DNA linked to disease but often could not name the causal variant, the target gene and the cell type; AlphaGenome 2 lines up all three at once. The method matters as much as the result: instead of averaging a tissue, the model predicts condition by condition, cell type by cell type — and it handles states it has never seen, such as cells after disease or vaccination (in a COVID-19 mRNA vaccine study it distinguished activated from resting T cells and found a variant linked to severe COVID-19 disrupts gene activity more strongly in the activated state). From tissue averages to cellular precision, this is a generational step for genomic AI.

## The good it brings

Disease mechanisms can be explained cell by cell for the first time: researchers can find causal variants faster and pin down exactly which cell type a drug should target, instead of searching across whole tissues — and the bar for rare-disease and complex-trait research falls with it. Two caveats stay attached: the model is for research use only and has not been validated for clinical or diagnostic use, so ordinary people cannot benefit from it directly yet; and every performance figure is DeepMind's own, with the technical paper a preprint that has not been peer-reviewed.
