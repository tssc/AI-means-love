### What happened
Google announced Gemini 4 Carbon, its next frontier model, described as its strongest model for cybersecurity and long-horizon work. It builds on Gemini 4 Argon, launched two weeks earlier. Google says Carbon ranks #1 on version 5.0 of the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index at 59, and leads three external cybersecurity benchmarks, evaluated without guardrails: 88.2% on SEC-bench Pro for finding real vulnerabilities, 54.8% on ExploitGym for validating them through exploitation, and 71.0% on CWE-bench for patching at the root cause. Rollout is deliberately gated: a subset of cyber defenders gets access first, then additional trusted partners through the Fairwind Program, while Google takes part in the U.S. government's voluntary pre-release model-testing process; developers, businesses and consumers follow "as soon as possible", with no public date. Introductory pricing matches Argon: $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens, with cached input at 95% off.

### Why it matters
Frontier competition turns to defense first: the strongest new model is handed to the people who patch the software everyone else relies on, before it reaches general users — and its headline claim is not general chat quality but finding, proving and fixing real flaws. Google also reports safety gains over Argon from its own testing: more than 2x uplift in generating secure code, high-severity misalignment alerts down by almost 80% in live coding with its own teams, and 0.0% attack success on Gray Swan's Indirect Prompt Injection Benchmark (Argon: 0.7%). Those figures are Google's own and have not been independently reproduced; the external benchmark scores were run without guardrails, as Google states.

### The good it brings
If the defense numbers hold up outside Google, ordinary users benefit indirectly: the software, browsers and cloud services they use every day get found flaws patched faster and more securely, by defenders rather than attackers first. The gated rollout itself is the other good: a frontier model with dual-use security capability is tested with governments and defenders before wide release, instead of after.

### 发生了什么
Google 发布下一代前沿模型 Gemini 4 Carbon，称其为在网络安全与长程任务上最强的模型，基于两周前发布的 Gemini 4 Argon。Google 称 Carbon 在 Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v5.0 上以 59 分排名第一，并在三项外部网络安全基准上领先（按官方说明，外部基准测试时未加护栏）：SEC-bench Pro 找真实漏洞 88.2%、ExploitGym 以利用验证风险 54.8%、CWE-bench 根因修复 71.0%。上线有意设限：先给一部分网络安全防御者，再通过 Fairwind Program 逐步扩大到受信合作伙伴，同时 Google 参与美国政府的自愿发布前模型测试；开发者、企业与普通用户随后「尽快」开放，暂无公开日期。起步价与 Argon 相同：每百万输入 token 2 美元、输出 10 美元，缓存输入 95% 折扣。

### 为什么值得被历史记住
前沿竞争第一次把「防御优先」摆到台前：最强的新模型先交到给全世界软件打补丁的人手里，而不是先交到普通用户手里；它的头条卖点也不是通用聊天质量，而是发现、验证并修复真实漏洞。Google 自测还报告了相对 Argon 的安全改进：生成安全代码成功率提升 2 倍以上、内部团队实测中高严重度失齐警报下降近 80%、Gray Swan 间接提示注入基准攻击成功率 0.0%（Argon 为 0.7%）。以上均为 Google 自测，尚未经独立复现；外部基准分数按官方说明是在无护栏条件下测得。

### 它带来的善
如果这些防御数字在 Google 之外站得住，普通人间接受益：每天用的软件、浏览器与云服务，漏洞会被防御者更快、更稳妥地修好，而不是先被攻击者利用。设限上线本身也是善：一个具有两用安全能力的前沿模型，先与政府和防御者一起测试，再广泛放开，而不是先放开再补救。
