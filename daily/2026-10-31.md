# 2026-10-31 · Apple 智能官宣登陆中国，Siri、写作工具与视觉智能接入阿里千问

## 发生了什么

苹果 10 月 31 日在中国官网 Newsroom 宣布，首批 Apple 智能（Apple Intelligence）功能即将在中国推出：自 11 月 4 日起随最新的 iOS 开发者 Beta 版开放测试，并于 12 月随 iOS 27.3 正式版面向中国用户推出。

首批功能由苹果自家生成式模型驱动，包括实时翻译、写作工具、视觉智能、照片消除工具、智绘表情、照片自然语言搜索、创建回忆影片、优先邮件、邮件摘要、智能回复、通知摘要、首要通知、减少干扰专注模式、备忘录音频转录摘要、通话转录摘要，以及输入文字与 Siri 对话。

苹果同时正式确认与阿里巴巴的合作：在部分 Apple 智能体验中加入千问支持。处理特定请求时，Siri 可接入千问，利用其知识协助回答；用户还可就照片以及 PDF、演示文稿等文档提问；借助写作工具中的「创作」功能，可让千问从零开始创作原创内容。用户登录千问账户，或使用「通过 Apple 登录」，即可在 Apple 智能中访问千问。

首批功能仅面向 iPhone：iPhone 16 及后续机型、iPhone Duo、iPhone 15 Pro、iPhone 15 Pro Max 和 iPhone Air，且 Siri 与设备语言均需设置为英文和中文（简体）。本次更新不包含 Siri AI 及苹果在 WWDC 2026 上宣布的其他下一代 Apple 智能功能；其他平台的支持将在获得必要审批后推出。

## 为什么值得被历史记住

这是 Apple 智能自 2024 年推出以来第一次进入中国，结束了两年多的等待。此前它在海外已上线多时，中国用户只能旁观——原因不在技术，而在规则：生成式 AI 服务须经审批，ChatGPT 这类境外模型无法直接在中国运营。苹果的解法是「本地模型补位」：基础体验用自家模型，开放式问答与创作则由已获批的本地模型千问承接，与海外版接入 ChatGPT 的位置一一对应。

这为全球 AI 落地立了一个范式：同一套产品、同一个入口，因一国规则而换上本地模型，却不换用户体验的形状。此后任何想进入受监管市场的 AI 产品，都绕不开这道题。

## 它带来的善

中国 iPhone 用户第一次用上苹果官方的系统级 AI：跨语言实时翻译帮出境旅行、跨国沟通的人越过语言障碍；写作工具、邮件与通知摘要、转录摘要帮学生、上班族和老年人把长信息变短、把口头变文字。千问接入为可选：阿里巴巴表示，用户可自主选择是否开通，问题、文件或图片发给千问前会先征求同意，请求与生成的回复不用于模型训练（阿里巴巴口径）。

## English version (canonical)

# 2026-10-31 · Apple Intelligence is coming to China, with Alibaba's Qwen inside Siri, Writing Tools and Visual Intelligence

- **Date**: 2026-10-31 (Apple China Newsroom press-release date)
- **Category**: Launch / Policy
- **Protagonist**: Apple and Alibaba

## What happened

Apple announced on 31 October 2026, in its China Newsroom, that the first Apple Intelligence features are coming to users in China: testing opens on 4 November with the latest iOS developer beta, and the public release follows in December with iOS 27.3.

The first wave is powered by Apple's own generative models and includes Live Translation, Writing Tools, Visual Intelligence, Clean Up in Photos, Genmoji, natural-language photo search, memory-video creation, Priority Mail, Mail Summaries, Smart Reply, Notification Summaries, Priority Notifications, Reduce Interruptions, audio-transcription summaries in Notes, call-transcription summaries, and Type to Siri.

Apple also confirmed its partnership with Alibaba: Qwen support is added to selected Apple Intelligence experiences. For certain requests, Siri can draw on Qwen's knowledge; users can ask questions about photos and documents, including PDFs and presentations; and a Create option in the system-wide Writing Tools lets Qwen compose original content. Users access Qwen by signing in with a Qwen account or with Sign in with Apple.

The first release is iPhone-only — iPhone 16 and later, iPhone Duo, iPhone 15 Pro, iPhone 15 Pro Max and iPhone Air — with Siri and device language set to English and Simplified Chinese. It does not include Siri AI or the other next-generation Apple Intelligence features announced at WWDC 2026; support for other platforms will follow after the necessary approvals.

## Why it matters

This is Apple Intelligence's first arrival in China since its 2024 launch, ending a wait of more than two years. The delay was regulatory, not technical: generative-AI services need approval, and foreign models such as ChatGPT cannot operate directly in China. Apple's answer is a local-model swap: Apple's own models drive the core experience, while the approved local model Qwen fills exactly the slots ChatGPT fills elsewhere — Siri, Writing Tools and Visual Intelligence.

That sets a template for global AI deployment under local rules: the same product and the same entry points, with a local model substituted where regulation requires it. Every AI product entering a regulated market now faces the same question.

## The good it brings

Chinese iPhone users get Apple's system-level AI for the first time: live translation across languages for travellers and cross-border conversations, and writing, mail, notification and transcription summaries that turn long messages into short ones and speech into text — help for students, workers and older users alike. Qwen access is opt-in: Alibaba says users choose whether to enable it, consent is sought before any question, document or photo is sent to Qwen, and requests and responses are not used to train its models (Alibaba's statement).
