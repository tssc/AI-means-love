## 发生了什么

OpenAI 推出 ChatGPT Wallet——让智能体替用户完成购买的钱包。用户一次添加银行卡，之后每次购买前先核对商品、总额与所选卡片，再以 Face ID 或指纹等安全检查批准；批准后支付网络发一张替代真实卡号的支付令牌（含卡号、安全码与有效期），商家与智能体都看不到真实卡号。OpenAI 称这是「首个让智能体在不接触用户真实卡号的前提下付款、且每笔购买都要求安全检查的钱包」，同时保留原有银行卡的积分奖励与合规争议保障。

钱包与 American Express、Mastercard、Visa 合作建成，初期仅通过 dots（OpenAI 9 月推出的常驻智能体）提供给美国 ChatGPT Pro 订阅用户（18 岁以上、持合规美国发卡行卡），逐步推广。OpenAI 博客称每周有 12 亿人使用 ChatGPT，其中许多人用它研究商品、比较选择、决定买什么——Wallet 让智能体把「买」这一步也走完。同日 OpenAI 还发布《Commerce Partner Playbook》，指导商家让网站接受智能体付款：认领商家信息、上传商品 feed、用 Web Bot Auth 让 CDN 与防火墙识别已签名的智能体请求、与支付与风控商协作减少误拒。反欺诈合作方包括 Forter、Riskified、Sardine 与 Signifyd，支付服务商包括 Adyen、Checkout.com 与 Stripe。OpenAI 表示，未来用户可通过设定消费上限、购买品类或时间段，把购买完全委托给智能体，并以生物识别检查与明确授权记录作为保障。

## 为什么值得被历史记住

这是智能体从「帮你挑」跨进「替你付」的分水岭：找商品、比价格、下单付款第一次在同一个智能体里闭环，而付钱这一步恰恰是过去所有购物智能体卡住的地方。Wallet 的做法不是让智能体保管卡号，而是把卡号换成支付网络签发的令牌、每笔购买都由用户本人刷脸或指纹批准——把「谁能花钱」的决定权留在用户手里，同时让商家沿用现有结账系统、保留与客户的关系与定价、履约、退货的主导权。三大卡组织同时入场，说明这不是一家公司的实验，而是支付行业对「智能体结账」这个新渠道的共同下注。个人智能体时代的叙事也因此补上关键一块：2026 年 9 月 Muse 以个人智能体形态出现，11 月智能体开始替人花钱——助手从对话者变成了能行动的代理人。

## 它带来的善

对普通人最直接的好处是：不用把卡号交给智能体或商家，也不用在结账页反复填表，就能让智能体帮你把买东西这件事办完；原有积分与争议保障不变，买到残次品仍可申诉。对不擅长操作结账流程的人——例如视力不佳的老人——把购买委托给可信的智能体、每笔仍由本人刷脸批准，是一道新的无障碍门槛。但要保留提醒：上线初期只有美国 Pro 订阅用户能用，绝大多数用户还摸不到；商家接受度、误拒率与实际安全性都要经过真实使用检验；「首个」是 OpenAI 的自称，Visa 与 Mastercard 早在 2025 年 4 月就已发布各自的智能体支付方案，Wallet 是第一个装进主流聊天机器人的消费级落地。

## English version (canonical)

# 2026-11-14 · OpenAI launches ChatGPT Wallet: agents can pay for you for the first time

- **Date**: 2026-11-14
- **Category**: Release
- **Protagonist**: OpenAI (with American Express, Mastercard and Visa)

## What happened

OpenAI launched ChatGPT Wallet, a wallet that lets an AI agent complete purchases on a user's behalf. The user adds a card once; before each purchase they review the items, the total and the selected card, then approve it with a security check such as Face ID or a fingerprint scan. After approval, the payment network issues a payment token — a substitute for the user's actual card details, with a card number, security code and expiration date — so neither the business nor the agent ever sees the real card number. OpenAI calls it "the first wallet that lets agents pay without access to users' actual card details and that requires a security check for every purchase," while preserving existing card rewards and protections for eligible disputes.

The wallet was built in partnership with American Express, Mastercard and Visa. It begins rolling out through dots (OpenAI's always-on agents, introduced in September) to ChatGPT Pro subscribers in the US aged 18 and older with eligible US-issued cards, expanding gradually. OpenAI says 1.2 billion people use ChatGPT each week, many of them to research products, compare options and decide what to buy — Wallet lets their agents finish the purchase too. Alongside the wallet, OpenAI published a Commerce Partner Playbook guiding businesses to accept agent payments: claim business details, upload a product feed, use Web Bot Auth so CDNs and firewalls recognize signed agent requests, and work with payment and risk providers to reduce false declines. Fraud-prevention partners include Forter, Riskified, Sardine and Signifyd; payment service providers include Adyen, Checkout.com and Stripe. OpenAI says that over time users will be able to delegate purchases entirely to their agent by setting parameters such as spending limits, purchase categories or time periods, with biometric checks and clear records of authorization as safeguards.

## Why it matters

This is the watershed where agents move from "help you choose" to "pay for you": finding a product, comparing prices and completing the purchase close the loop inside a single agent, and payment was exactly the step where every shopping agent used to stall. Wallet's answer is not to hand the agent the card number, but to replace it with a token issued by the payment network and to require the user to approve every purchase with a face or fingerprint — keeping the decision of who spends money in the user's hands, while letting businesses keep their existing checkout systems, customer relationships and control over pricing, fulfillment and returns. All three major card networks joining at once signals this is not one company's experiment but the payments industry's shared bet on agent checkout as a new channel. It also completes a missing piece of the personal-agent era: Muse arrived as a personal agent in September 2026, and by November agents were spending money for people — the assistant stops being a conversationalist and becomes an agent that acts.

## The good it brings

For ordinary people the direct benefit is simple: an agent can finish a purchase for you without your card number ever reaching the agent or the merchant, and without retyping payment details at checkout — while the card's rewards and dispute protections stay intact, so a defective item can still be challenged. For people who struggle with checkout flows — an older person with poor eyesight, for example — delegating a purchase to a trusted agent, with every transaction still approved by their own face or fingerprint, opens a new door to accessibility. Two caveats stay attached: at launch only US Pro subscribers can use it, so most users cannot touch it yet; and merchant acceptance, false-decline rates and real-world safety all have to be proven in use. "First" is OpenAI's own claim — Visa and Mastercard each published their own agent-payment products in April 2025 — Wallet is the first consumer-grade one built into a mainstream chatbot.
