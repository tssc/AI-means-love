## 发生了什么

月之暗面在 Hugging Face 公开 Kimi K3.1 Pro 的完整权重（仓库 moonshotai/Kimi-K3.1-Pro），无访问门槛，任何人可直接下载。模型规格与 10 月 23 日发布时一致：总参数 2.8 万亿、每 token 激活约 1040 亿、1M 上下文、原生多模态。权重以 MXFP4 量化（量化感知训练，激活为 MXFP8）打包为 96 个 Safetensors 分片，总量约 1.56TB；首日即给出 vLLM、SGLang、TokenSpeed 三个推理引擎的部署支持。模型卡附有完整评测表，均为月之暗面自测，未独立复现。

许可为 MIT 风格的 Kimi K3.1 Pro License。附加条件只针对大体量主体：以模型即服务形式提供且年收入超过 2000 万美元的，需另签协议；月活超过 1 亿或月收入超过 2000 万美元的产品，须显著署名「Kimi K3.1 Pro」。普通研究、使用与商业部署不受这些附加条件影响。

## 为什么值得被历史记住

这一条记的不是又一个模型，而是一次承诺的兑现。10 月 23 日 K3.1 Pro 发布时，月之暗面把「11 月 4 日前开放完整权重」写进了公告；11 月 4 日，权重准时出现在公开仓库里。3T 级旗舰的完整权重一旦落地，模型的身份就变了：从只能通过 API 租用的服务，变成任何人可以下载、归档、检验的文件——独立评测、复现、私有化部署、微调都从「需要许可」变成「只需要算力」。同一天入线的 Hy4 是另一种顺序（服务先行、权重待放），两条并排，正好记下 2026 年前沿模型两种不同的开放路径。

## 它带来的善

对普通人和中小团队而言，开放权重意味着能力不再只能按月租用：数据敏感的机构可以在本地私有部署，研究者可以逐层检验模型而不是只看厂商分数，任何人都可以站在 3T 级模型之上继续建造。而按时兑现开放承诺本身，也在为行业积累一种稀缺品——对「会开源」这句话的信任。

## English version (canonical)

# 2026-11-04 · Moonshot releases the Kimi K3.1 Pro weights: the "by Nov 4" promise lands on its deadline

- **Date**: 2026-11-04 (public release day; the repo's first commit was Nov 2, see note in the Chinese version)
- **Category**: Open source / models
- **Protagonist**: Moonshot AI

## What happened

Moonshot AI published the full weights of Kimi K3.1 Pro on Hugging Face (moonshotai/Kimi-K3.1-Pro), publicly downloadable with no access gate. The specs match the Oct 23 launch: 2.8 trillion total parameters, about 104B activated per token, a 1M-token context window, native multimodality. The weights ship as 96 MXFP4 Safetensors shards (quantization-aware training, MXFP8 activations) totalling about 1.56TB, with deployment support for vLLM, SGLang and TokenSpeed from day one. The model card's full benchmark table is Moonshot's own testing, not independently reproduced.

The licence is the MIT-style Kimi K3.1 Pro License. Its extra terms bind only very large operators: providing the model as a service with annual revenue above US$20M requires a separate agreement, and products with more than 100M monthly active users or more than US$20M in monthly revenue must display prominent "Kimi K3.1 Pro" attribution. Ordinary research, use and commercial deployment are unaffected.

## Why it matters

This entry records not another model, but a promise kept. At the Oct 23 launch, Moonshot wrote "full weights by Nov 4" into its announcement; on Nov 4, the weights appeared in a public repository. Once a 3T-class flagship's full weights land, the model's nature changes: from a service you can only rent through an API to a file anyone can download, archive and inspect — independent evaluation, reproduction, private deployment and fine-tuning stop needing permission and start needing only compute. Set beside the same day's Hy4 entry (service first, weights still to come), the pair records the two different sequences by which frontier models opened up in 2026.

## The good it brings

For ordinary people and small teams, open weights mean capability no longer has to be rented by the month: institutions with sensitive data can deploy privately on-premises, researchers can inspect the model layer by layer instead of trusting vendor scores, and anyone can build on top of a 3T-class model. And a weights promise kept on schedule accumulates something scarce for the whole field — trust in the words "we will open it."
