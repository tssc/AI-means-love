## 发生了什么

Google 发布 Gemini 4 Hydrogen——Gemini 4 家族的第三款模型，也是第一款面向所有人的成员。Carbon（10 月 29 日）主攻网络安全与长程推理，Argon 主攻深度推理，Hydrogen 的定位是快速执行：日常编码与多步骤智能体工作。它取代 Gemini 3.8 Flash，成为 Google 各产品线的日常模型。官方数字：在默认 high 思考档位下，完成任务比 3.8 Flash 快 2.5 倍，Terminal-Bench 4.1 得分 57%（3.8 Flash 为 18.2%），编造内容减少 5 倍以上；Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index 默认档位 51 分，超过 Argon 的 50 分，最高 xhigh 档位达 54 分。模型提供 low、medium、high（默认）、xhigh 四档思考级别，1 百万 token 上下文，输入每百万 token 1.50 美元、输出 7.50 美元，全上下文统一价。

Hydrogen 同日向全球所有用户推出：它是 Google 搜索 AI Mode 的新默认模型，并处理最难的 AI Overviews 问题——Google 搜索产品副总裁 Robby Stein 称这是「迄今为止搜索最大的一次模型升级」。同时上线 Gemini 应用、Google AI Studio 全部层级、Gemini API、Gemini for business、Android Studio，以及 Google AI Pro/Ultra 订阅者的 Antigravity。Antigravity 还同步推出 Orchestrator：同一开发会话内在 Carbon 与 Hydrogen 之间动态切换，Terminal-Bench 4.0 上接近 Carbon 的任务成功率，同时少用 24% 配额、快 19%。

## 为什么值得被历史记住

这是 Gemini 4 家族补齐的一块：Argon 负责深想、Carbon 负责守卫、Hydrogen 负责快干——三层分工第一次完整落地，而 Orchestrator 把「多模型协作」本身做成了产品：规划用大模型、执行与核验用快模型，成本与速度同时优化。更重要的是受众：Hydrogen 是第一款免费进入搜索与 Gemini 应用的 Gemini 4 模型——全球数十亿搜索用户无需付费就用上了推理型智能体模型，复杂问题（多部分、错别字、高度具体的要求）由它拆解回答，查询扇出（query fan-out）一次跑多个子查询、返回更多来源链接，回答也更可视化。旗舰能力第一次以「默认配置」而不是「付费层级」出现在世界上最常用的信息入口。

## 它带来的善

普通人不用订阅就能在搜索里得到更准确、更完整的复杂问题答案，也能在 Gemini 应用里少来回几轮就把邮件、学习资料、文档写好；开发者则以每百万输入 token 1.50 美元的价格用到接近前沿的执行能力，小团队也能跑多步骤智能体工作。安全上也有实在改进：与 3.8 Flash 相比，智能体越权减少最多 81%、服务不足减少最多 79%、错位行为与任务走捷径减少 12 倍，HealthBench Pro 上的误拒率低于 0.4%——这些改进随免费版一起到达最广大的用户。但须保留提醒：全部数字均为 Google 自报；Google 自家对比也承认它在科学与数学等维度落后于同级对手；搜索回答的实际准确度仍待真实使用检验。

## English version (canonical)

# 2026-11-13 · Google releases Gemini 4 Hydrogen: Gemini 4 reaches everyone through Search

- **Date**: 2026-11-13
- **Category**: Release
- **Protagonist**: Google (Google DeepMind)

## What happened

Google released Gemini 4 Hydrogen, the third model in the Gemini 4 family and the first built for everyone. Carbon (October 29) handles cybersecurity and long-horizon reasoning, Argon handles deep reasoning, and Hydrogen is the fast-execution tier for everyday coding and multi-step agentic work. It replaces Gemini 3.8 Flash as the everyday model across Google's products. Google's figures: at its default high thinking level it finishes tasks 2.5 times faster than 3.8 Flash, scores 57% on Terminal-Bench 4.1 against 18.2% for 3.8 Flash, and cuts fabricated claims by more than 5x; it scores 51 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index at the default setting, above Argon's 50, and reaches 54 at its highest xhigh setting. The model offers four thinking levels — low, medium, high (default) and a new xhigh tier — with a 1-million-token context window priced flat at $1.50 per million input tokens and $7.50 per million output tokens.

Hydrogen rolled out globally the same day to all users: it is the new default model in Google Search's AI Mode and handles the hardest AI Overviews questions — Google Search VP of Product Robby Stein calls it "the biggest model upgrade to Search yet." It also reached the Gemini app, all tiers of Google AI Studio, the Gemini API, Gemini for business, Android Studio, and Google Antigravity for Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers. Antigravity also gained Orchestrator, which switches dynamically between Carbon and Hydrogen inside one development session: on Terminal-Bench 4.0 it reached near-Carbon task success rates while using 24% less quota and finishing 19% faster.

## Why it matters

This completes the Gemini 4 family: Argon thinks deep, Carbon guards, Hydrogen executes fast — and Orchestrator turns multi-model collaboration itself into a product, with the big model planning and the fast model executing and verifying, saving cost and time at once. The audience matters more: Hydrogen is the first Gemini 4 model free inside Search and the Gemini app, so billions of search users get a reasoning agent model by default, not as a paid tier — complex questions (multi-part, typo-heavy, highly specific) are broken down and answered piece by piece, query fan-out runs several sub-queries at once and returns a wider range of source links, and answers come back more visual. Frontier capability arrives as the default setting of the world's most-used information interface.

## The good it brings

Ordinary people get more accurate, more complete answers to complex questions in Search without a subscription, and fewer back-and-forth rounds in the Gemini app to finish an email, study guide or document; developers get near-frontier execution at $1.50 per million input tokens, so small teams can run multi-step agent work too. Safety improves in ways that reach the widest audience with the free version: agentic overreach down by up to 81% and under-service by up to 79% against 3.8 Flash, misaligned actions and task shortcutting cut 12x, and false refusals on HealthBench Pro below 0.4%. Two caveats stay attached: every figure is Google's own, not independently reproduced; and Google's own comparison table concedes Hydrogen trails rivals such as Claude Sonnet 5.5 and GPT-6.1 Terra on science, math, computer use and Terminal-Bench 4.1 — and real-world Search accuracy still has to be proven in use.
