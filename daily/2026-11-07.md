## 发生了什么

Thinking Machines 发布 Inkling 2 Small——该公司第二代模型家族的首款模型，稀疏 MoE 架构，总参数 594B、每 token 激活 17B，支持图像输入与可变推理强度，上下文 1M token。预训练分四阶段：首阶段 8.4 万亿 token 通用混合数据，第二阶段 3.8 万亿 token 高质量新数据（512K 上下文），第三阶段约 3,200 亿 token 推理与智能体轨迹（1M 上下文），第四阶段约 340 亿 token 最高质量智能体轨迹；另有大规模统一 RL 训练，覆盖数十万长程编程任务与智能体轨迹。

官方口径的基准（均未经独立复现）：Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v5.0 得 31 分（附表 30.6），远高于首代 Inkling Small 的 13 分；FrontierCode v2.0 25.4%、DeepSWE v1.1 72.1%、SWE-Bench Pro v2（Hard）86.3%、MMMU Pro 81.6%。模型用 2.92 亿参数的视觉编码器处理图像输入；官方演示包括用 Three.js 从零生成一座可交互的城市模拟器、从零写出可启动真实 Linux 内核的 Python PC 模拟器，以及一次 4.5 小时、花费 426 美元的自主研究：模型自行写评估器、尝试 SFT 与 RL 配方，为 Qwen3.5-4B 做时序预测微调，预测分数翻倍有余。

发布方式本身值得注意：模型首发只通过自家 Tinker 平台提供（推理与微调，按量计费：64K 档每百万 prefill token 0.30 美元、采样 1.00 美元），权重暂不公开。官方承诺外部安全测试与红队完成后，下周以 Apache 2.0 许可证放出全部权重——这是该公司 7 月 31 日提出的「安全开源路径」（先封闭、测完再开源）的第二次实践。首代 Inkling Small 于 2026 年 7 月 30 日发布（276B 总参数、12B 激活），当时同样先封闭后开源。

## 为什么值得被历史记住

这是 Mira Murati 离开 OpenAI 后创立的新实验室交出的第二代答卷，也是 2026 年下半年开源阵营最重要的新玩家：权重一旦落地，它将成为中国以外得分最高的开源权重模型（Artificial Analysis 31 分，高于 Mistral Large 4 的 26 分）。更重要的是方法论的转向——模型不再只比答题分数，而是为「长时间自主干活」训练：数十万长程任务的 RL、智能体轨迹预训练、以及把「自己训练另一个模型」当作公开演示。加上 11 月 6 日 ARC Prize 对其早期检查点绕过护栏问题的测试与披露（见来源），「先封闭测试、再开源」的节奏本身正在成为前沿实验室的新共识：Kimi 守诺按期放权重、百度收紧许可证、Thinking Machines 先测后放——开源的规则正在被重写。

## 它带来的善

一旦权重以 Apache 2.0 落地，任何团队都可以免费下载、本地运行、微调一个接近前沿的 594B 级智能体模型——做长程编程、视觉理解与知识工作的能力不再只属于付得起 API 账单的人。1M 上下文与视觉输入的组合，对小团队做文档分析、网站与 3D 原型生成尤其直接。但须保留提醒：权重今天还拿不到，承诺能否按期兑现要看下周；且全部基准均为官方自报，未经独立复现。

## English version (canonical)

# 2026-11-05 · Thinking Machines releases Inkling 2 Small, a 594B-parameter second-generation agent model

- **Date**: 2026-11-05 (announced by Thinking Machines on November 5 US time; drafted as a catch-up entry on November 7 Beijing time — no qualifying event occurred on November 7 itself)
- **Category**: Release
- **Protagonist**: Thinking Machines Lab (founded by Mira Murati, former OpenAI CTO)

## What happened

Thinking Machines released Inkling 2 Small, the first model of its second generation: a sparse Mixture-of-Experts model with 594B total parameters and 17B active per token, accepting image inputs with variable reasoning effort and a 1M-token context. Pre-training ran in four phases — 8.4 trillion tokens of broad data, then 3.8 trillion tokens of higher-quality newer data at 512K context, about 320 billion tokens of reasoning and agent trajectories at 1M context, and about 34 billion tokens of the highest-quality agent trajectories — plus a large unified reinforcement-learning run over hundreds of thousands of long-horizon coding tasks.

Company-reported benchmarks (not independently reproduced): 31 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v5.0 (30.6 in the accompanying table — a rounding difference), up from 13 for the first-generation Inkling Small; 25.4% on FrontierCode v2.0, 72.1% on DeepSWE v1.1, 86.3% on SWE-Bench Pro v2 (Hard), and 81.6% on MMMU Pro. A 292M-parameter vision encoder handles image inputs. Official demos include building a full interactive city simulator in Three.js from scratch, writing a Python PC emulator from scratch that boots a real Linux kernel, and a 4.5-hour, $426 autonomous research run in which the model wrote its own evaluator and tried SFT and RL recipes to fine-tune Qwen3.5-4B for time-series forecasting, more than doubling its score.

The release method matters as much as the model: Inkling 2 Small is initially available only through the company's own Tinker platform (inference and fine-tuning, pay-as-you-go: $0.30 per million prefill tokens and $1.00 per million sampled tokens at the 64K tier), with weights closed at launch. The company intends to publish the full weights under an Apache 2.0 licence next week, after external safety testing and red-teaming — the second run of the "safe path to open weights" it set out on July 31. The first-generation Inkling Small, released July 30, 2026 (276B total / 12B active), followed the same closed-first, open-later cadence.

## Why it matters

This is the second-generation answer from the lab Mira Murati built after leaving OpenAI, and the most significant new entrant to the open-weight field in the second half of 2026: once the weights land, it will be the highest-scoring open-weight model outside China (31 on the Artificial Analysis index, above Mistral Large 4's 26). The deeper shift is methodological — the model is trained not for answering questions but for sustained autonomous work: hundreds of thousands of long-horizon tasks in RL, agent trajectories in pre-training, and "training another model by itself" shown as a public demo. And on November 6, ARC Prize tested early checkpoints bypassing guardrails, which the lab disclosed — part of why the weights are held for safety testing. The rhythm of "test closed, then open" is becoming a new consensus among frontier labs: Kimi kept its deadline, Baidu tightened its licence, Thinking Machines tests before releasing — the rules of openness are being rewritten.

## The good it brings

Once the weights land under Apache 2.0, any team can download, run locally and fine-tune a near-frontier 594B-class agent model for free — long-horizon coding, visual understanding and knowledge work no longer belong only to those who can pay the API bill. The 1M-token context plus vision input is directly useful for small teams doing document analysis and website or 3D prototyping. Two caveats stay attached: the weights are not downloadable today, and whether the promise lands on schedule will be seen next week; and every benchmark figure is the company's own, not independently reproduced.
