# 2026-11-04 · 腾讯发布 Hy4 正式版：首次加入视觉理解与电脑操作

> 备稿说明：本稿已获 Han 批准，2026-11-05 提交（时间线第 57 条，排在 2026-10-31 Super-agent 行之后；11-02 稿（美团 LongCat-2.5）与 11-03 稿（Anthropic RSI 预警）仍待批准，日后若批准，将按日期插入本条之前）。另需如实说明：本次为正式版服务发布，模型权重尚未放出（官方口径为「未来几天内」发布权重与系统卡），故本条记为「发布」而非「开源权重落地」；路透社标题称其为开源模型，系按其后续开源计划表述，与官方「权重待发布」的实际状态有出入，以官方为准。

## 发生了什么

腾讯混元 11 月 4 日发布新一代大语言模型 Hy4 正式版，是 2026 年 8 月 Hy4 preview 之后的正式版。相比 preview，正式版大幅扩展预训练与后训练规模，并首次引入两项能力：视觉理解（可处理细粒度图像、复杂图表、含图数学推理）与电脑操作（可同时操作命令行与图形界面，并针对 Blender、CAD 等专业软件做了专门训练）。模型已上线腾讯云 TokenHub、WorkBuddy、CodeBuddy、元宝、ima、企业微信智能助手（Dayuan），API 并经 OpenRouter、Hermes、OpenCode 等第三方平台提供；更轻量的 Hy4 Flash 计划随后发布，主打成本与速度。权重与系统卡尚未放出，官方口径为未来几天内发布，并将联合 vLLM、SGLang 社区提供生态支持。

安全口径须按官方原文写准：Hy4 训练使用防御性安全数据，优先概念验证（PoC）开发与漏洞修复；逆向工程与渗透测试是「为分析目的」纳入的，被明确排除的是与漏洞利用相关的攻击性材料。腾讯玄武实验室与科恩实验室的评估称，在所评估的密码逆向、反混淆、漏洞利用等领域，未发现 Hy4 攻击性网络安全能力方面存在重大问题。路透社当日报道将其概括为「过滤了漏洞开发、逆向工程、渗透测试」，与官方原文有出入，以官方为准。另，官方内部评估数字（未经独立复现）：幻觉率从 preview 的 12.95% 降至 7.2%，GameDev 较 preview 提升 19.1%、GameLogic 提升 9.8%。

## 为什么值得被历史记住

Hy4 是混元系列第一次把「看」与「动手」合进同一款旗舰模型：不再只回答问题，而是能看图、读表、进而直接在电脑上跨应用把活干完——从办公协作、金融分析到前端与游戏开发，模型与产品的协同设计成为发布本身的一部分。它也是 2026 年 10 月以来「能力与克制同框」的又一例证：继月之暗面主动剔除高危网络攻击语料、Anthropic 把 RSI 写进招股文件之后，腾讯在正式版发布当天就把网络与生物安全保障写进发布口径，并把权重发布推迟到系统卡就绪之后。开放路线正在从「先放出再说」转向「先讲清防护再放出」。

## 它带来的善

对普通用户与小团队而言，Hy4 把视觉助手与电脑助手合二为一：给它一张图表、一份文档，它能接着分析、制表、建 3D 场景、写可运行的前端与游戏逻辑，而不必在多个工具间来回搬运。防御优先的训练口径则意味着，普通人拿到手的开放模型在设计上就更难被顺手改造成攻击工具——能力下放的同时，作恶门槛没有同等下放。但须保留提醒：在权重真正放出之前，普通开发者只能通过服务与 API 使用，离线自托管仍待观察。

## English version (canonical)

# 2026-11-04 · Tencent releases Hy4: vision and computer use for the first time

- **Date**: 2026-11-04
- **Category**: Release
- **Protagonist**: Tencent Hunyuan (Tencent Hy)

## What happened

On November 4, Tencent Hunyuan released Hy4, the full version of its latest-generation large language model, following the Hy4 preview of August 2026. Against the preview, the full release substantially scales pre-training and post-training, and adds two capabilities for the first time: visual understanding (fine-grained image processing, complex chart analysis, mathematical reasoning with images) and computer use (operating both command-line and graphical interfaces, with specialized training on professional software such as Blender and CAD). Hy4 is live across Tencent Cloud TokenHub, WorkBuddy, CodeBuddy, Yuanbao, ima and the WeCom AI assistant (Dayuan), with API access via third-party platforms including OpenRouter, Hermes and OpenCode; a lighter Hy4 Flash focused on cost and speed is planned soon. Model weights and a system card are not out yet — Tencent says they will follow in the coming days, with ecosystem support developed alongside the vLLM and SGLang communities.

The safety wording must follow the official text: Hy4 is trained on defensive security data, prioritizing proof-of-concept development and vulnerability remediation; reverse engineering and penetration testing are included for analysis purposes, while offensive materials related to exploitation are explicitly excluded from training. Assessments by Tencent's Xuanwu Lab and Keen Security Lab found no significant concerns about Hy4's offensive cybersecurity capabilities in the areas evaluated. Reuters' same-day summary described this as filtering out exploit development, reverse engineering and penetration testing, which differs from the official text and is not followed here. Company figures, not independently reproduced: hallucination rate down from 12.95% in the preview to 7.2%, and GameDev up 19.1% and GameLogic up 9.8% against the preview.

## Why it matters

Hy4 is the first Hunyuan flagship to combine seeing and doing in one model: it no longer only answers questions, but can look at a chart or a document and then carry work through on the computer itself, across applications — from office collaboration and financial analysis to front-end and game development, with model-product co-design as part of the release itself. It is also the latest case, after Moonshot's removal of high-risk cyber-attack data in October and Anthropic's RSI disclosure in its prospectus, of capability and restraint arriving in the same announcement: Tencent put cybersecurity and biosafety safeguards into the release wording and held weights back until the system card is ready. The open road is shifting from "release first, explain later" to "explain the safeguards first, then release."

## The good it brings

For ordinary users and small teams, Hy4 merges a visual assistant and a computer assistant: hand it a chart or a document and it can analyze, tabulate, build a 3D scene or write working front-end and game logic without shuttling work between tools. A defence-first training stance means the open model reaching ordinary hands is designed to be harder to repurpose casually as an attack tool — capability is handed down without handing down an equal ease of misuse. One caveat stays attached: until weights are actually released, ordinary developers can only use Hy4 through services and APIs; offline self-hosting remains watch-and-wait.
