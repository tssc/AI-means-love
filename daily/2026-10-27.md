- **Date**: 2026-10-27 (Asia/Shanghai; some UTC listings show 2026-10-26)
- **Category**: Open source
- **Protagonist**: Baidu

### What happened
Baidu released Unlimited OCR 2, an open-source vision-language model for parsing very long documents. The 3.34B-parameter model reads books, filings and reports of hundreds of pages in a single request, under the MIT license. Its core change is SR-SWA (Sparse Reference Sliding Window Attention): attention is compressed into a fixed 512-token reference set, so per-step decoding cost stays nearly flat as documents grow.

### Why it matters
Long documents were a quiet bottleneck: page-by-page OCR loses tables that span pages, cross-references and reading order. Unlimited OCR 2 keeps cross-page context in one pass — Baidu's own scores put it at 77.1 on MarathonDocBench (hundreds of pages), against 33.7 for the next model. All benchmark figures are Baidu's own, not independently verified.

### The good it brings
Archives, books, legal filings and medical records become searchable, usable text — for students, researchers, small teams and anyone digitizing paper history. Open weights under MIT mean any team can run it, not just rent it.

## ZH-CN

- **日期**：2026-10-27（北京时间；部分 UTC 来源标注为 10-26）
- **类别**：开源
- **主角**：百度

### 发生了什么
百度开源 Unlimited OCR 2，一个面向超长文档解析的视觉语言模型。模型约 33.4 亿参数、MIT 授权，一次请求可解析数百页的书籍、财报与报告。核心改进是 SR-SWA（稀疏参考滑动窗口注意力）：把注意力压缩进固定的 512 token 参考集，使单步解码成本随文档变长几乎不再增长。

### 为什么值得被历史记住
长文档一直是安静的瓶颈：逐页 OCR 会丢掉跨页表格、交叉引用与阅读顺序。Unlimited OCR 2 一次过读完整篇、保留跨页上下文——百度自测在数百页文档的 MarathonDocBench 上得 77.1 分，下一名仅 33.7 分。以上均为百度自测分数，未经独立验证。

### 它带来的善
档案、书籍、法律文件与病历变成可检索、可用的文本：学生、研究者、小团队，以及所有在做纸质历史数字化的人都能直接受益。MIT 开源意味着任何团队都能自己跑它，而不只是租用它。
