## 发生了什么

阿里通义千问团队正式发布 Qwen4-Max——Qwen4 系列的首款模型，也是迄今最强的千问模型。采用全新的稀疏 MoE 架构：2.4 万亿参数主干，每 token 激活 71B，另加 328B 的 N-gram Embedding 参数；原生视觉理解，100 万 token 上下文。官方数字：在自建的 Work360 行业任务基准（26 个一级领域、182 个二级、721 个三级细分领域）上得 52.69 分，前代 Qwen3.8-Max 为 47.74 分；单个任务平均成本从 4.43 美元降至 1.67 美元，降幅 62%；在编程基准 DeepSWE v1.1 上得 75.7 分，略高于 Claude Opus 5.5 的 74.2 分（阿里官方对比图数据）。

定价：国际站每百万 tokens 输入 1.50 美元、输出 4.50 美元，国内每百万 tokens 输入 10 元、输出 30 元。即日起上线 QwenCloud（Token Plan 与 API）、千问办公、Qoder 与 Qwen Studio。安全上同批升级：训练数据按任务目的过滤，保留漏洞验证、检测与修复等防御性内容，剔除用于提升攻击能力的内容；安全编码通过率从 22.0% 升至 31.8%；内部评审称未发现大规模网络安全风险能力。长程智能体功能 Live Delivery 让任务分段交付、用户随时介入，配套的 Qwen-Live-Delivery-Plugin 已开源。

## 为什么值得被历史记住

Qwen 是全球下载量最大的开源模型家族（Bloomberg 口径），这次旗舰却闭源——中国头部厂商的路线就此分化：旗舰闭源卖服务，小模型开源守生态；开放不再是「全家桶」，而是分层策略。同时 Qwen4-Max 把「智能体队友」做成了产品形态：Live Delivery 分段交付、24/7 长程任务、模型—工具—产品协同设计。更值得记的是两场演示：一次 48 小时无人干预的智能体运行，自主设计并合成了新型冷却剂分子，最终拿到实物样品；另一场针对阿尔茨海默病靶点 NLRP3 的药物研究，454 次工具调用、3,239 次计算评估、24 个候选分子，优先分子的结合亲和力估计约为参照物的 3.6 倍——智能体从写代码跨进了干湿实验闭环。

## 它带来的善

开发者与小团队以每百万输入 token 1.50 美元的价格用到接近前沿的执行能力，任务成本比上一代低六成以上；普通人通过千问 App、千问办公直接用上，不必订阅也能让智能体分段交付一份可用的工作成果。安全编码的改进让模型默认生成的代码少带漏洞，从源头缩小攻击面。但要保留提醒：旗舰闭源，下载与私有部署此路不通；全部数字均为阿里自报；药物与材料成果均为计算与实验室阶段，未经临床验证，离治病救人还很远。

## English version (canonical)

# 2026-11-16 · Alibaba releases Qwen4-Max: the world's most downloaded open-weight family ships its flagship closed

- **Date**: 2026-11-16
- **Category**: Release
- **Protagonist**: Alibaba's Qwen team

## What happened

Alibaba's Qwen team formally released Qwen4-Max, the first model of the Qwen4 series and the most capable Qwen model to date. It is built on a new sparse mixture-of-experts architecture: a 2.4-trillion-parameter backbone, 71B activated per token, plus an additional 328B N-gram Embedding parameters, with native vision and a one-million-token context window. On Alibaba's own numbers: on Work360, its benchmark of industry tasks spanning 26 top-level domains, 182 subdomains and 721 specialized fields, it scores 52.69 against 47.74 for Qwen3.8-Max; the average cost per task falls from $4.43 to $1.67, a 62% reduction; on the coding benchmark DeepSWE v1.1 it scores 75.7, just ahead of Claude Opus 5.5 at 74.2, according to Alibaba's published comparison charts.

Pricing is $1.50 per million input tokens and $4.50 per million output tokens on QwenCloud internationally (¥10/¥30 in China). It is available now through QwenCloud (Token Plan and API), QwenWork, Qoder and Qwen Studio. Safety is upgraded in the same release: training data is filtered by task purpose, keeping defensive cybersecurity content such as vulnerability verification, detection and remediation while removing data for offensive use; secure-coding pass rates rise from 22.0% to 31.8%; an internal review found no large-scale cybersecurity risk capability. Live Delivery ships long-running work in stages with the user able to step in at any point, and the Qwen-Live-Delivery-Plugin is open-sourced.

## Why it matters

Qwen is the world's most downloaded open-weight model family (Bloomberg), and this time its flagship is closed — the route splits for China's leading labs: the flagship is sold as a service behind an API while smaller models stay open. Openness stops being an all-or-nothing family trait and becomes a tiered strategy. Qwen4-Max is also sold as a teammate, not a chatbot: staged Live Delivery, 24/7 long-horizon work, and model–harness–product co-design. Two showcases make the point concrete: in a 48-hour unsupervised agent run, Qwen4 autonomously designed and synthesized a novel coolant molecule, ending with a physical sample; and in Alzheimer's drug research on the NLRP3 target, it made 454 tool calls and 3,239 computational evaluations across 24 candidate molecules, prioritizing one with an estimated 3.6× binding affinity against the reference — the agent crosses from writing code into a closed dry-and-wet-lab loop.

## The good it brings

Developers and small teams get near-frontier execution at $1.50 per million input tokens, with task costs down more than 60% against the previous generation; ordinary users reach it directly through the Qwen app and QwenWork, with staged delivery of usable work rather than a single take-it-or-leave-it answer. Better secure coding means the code the model writes by default carries fewer vulnerabilities, shrinking the attack surface at the source. Caveats stay attached: the flagship is closed, so downloading and self-hosting it is not an option; all figures are Alibaba's own, not independently reproduced; and the drug and materials results are computational and laboratory-stage, far from clinical validation or saving lives.
