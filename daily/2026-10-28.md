### What happened
Google DeepMind announced Genie 4, its new world model for real-time interactive simulation, as a research preview for trusted testers. Unlike a video generator that plays back a fixed clip, Genie 4 generates a world you move through and steer live: typed prompts can change the weather, move the camera, or add people and objects mid-simulation. It is DeepMind's first real-time world model to generate audio natively — music, ambient sound and interactive dialogue produced together with the scene. Gemini models sit in the loop: Gemini Omni anchors appearance and behaviour, and Gemini 3.8 Flash acts as a guiding agent, breaking goals into steps and checking each frame. Wider access is coming soon to Google AI Ultra subscribers in Project Genie and early-access partners of the Gemini API and Google AI Studio. All Genie 4 video and audio carries SynthID watermarking.

### Why it matters
The world model moves from silent, watch-only scenes to a place you can enter, talk to and change while it runs — the step that makes simulation usable for robot and agent training, film pre-visualization and game prototyping. TechCrunch reports scenes stay consistent for up to two minutes, double Genie 3's limit; that figure is reported, not stated in DeepMind's announcement. DeepMind itself is clear about the limits: details and object permanence can drift over longer sessions, and audio accuracy, legible text and spatial accuracy remain open research problems.

### The good it brings
Safe, infinitely variable practice worlds: robots and agents can be tested in imagined scenarios before they meet the real world, and creators can prototype a scene in seconds instead of building it first. The SynthID watermark keeps the generated worlds identifiable as AI-made.

### 发生了什么
Google DeepMind 发布新一代世界模型 Genie 4，以研究预览形式先向受信测试者开放。它不是播一段固定视频，而是实时生成一个可以走进去的世界：运行中输入文字，就能改天气、移镜头、加入人物或物体。Genie 4 是 DeepMind 首个原生生成音频的实时世界模型——音乐、环境音与互动对话与画面同步生成。Gemini 模型在环路里当「导演」：Gemini Omni 锚定外观与行为，Gemini 3.8 Flash 把目标拆成步骤、逐帧检查进度。后续将向 Project Genie 的 Google AI Ultra 订阅用户，以及 Gemini API 与 Google AI Studio 的早期合作伙伴开放。所有 Genie 4 生成的视频与音频都带有 SynthID 水印。

### 为什么值得被历史记住
世界模型从「无声、只能看」变成「能走进、能对话、能改动」的地方——这是它能用于机器人与智能体训练、影视预演、游戏原型的关键一步。TechCrunch 报道其场景一致性可达约 2 分钟，是 Genie 3 的两倍；该数字为媒体报道，官方公告未写明。DeepMind 自己也写明了局限：长会话中细节与物体恒常性仍会漂移，音频准确性、画面内文字可读性、空间准确性仍是待解的研究问题。

### 它带来的善
安全且无限可变 的练功场：机器人与智能体可以先在想象世界里试错，再走向真实世界；创作者不用先搭场景，几秒就能做出一个可走进去的原型。SynthID 水印让这些生成世界能被识别为 AI 制造。
