## 发生了什么

Anthropic 于美国时间 2026 年 11 月 18 日将首次公开募股定价为每股 83 美元（高于 70–78 美元的指导区间），出售 9.8 亿股 A 类普通股，其中公司出售 956,351,354 股、现有股东出售 23,648,646 股，募资 813 亿美元，超越 SpaceX 今年 6 月 750 亿美元的纪录，成为史上募资规模最大的 IPO；另有 260 亿美元的私募配售以同价认购。公司股票于美国时间 11 月 19 日在纳斯达克全球精选市场挂牌，代码 ANT，首笔交易 111.50 美元、较发行价高 34%，收报 97.17 美元、涨 17.07%，当日约 8.4 亿股换手；按发行价计算的完全稀释估值约 1.5 万亿美元，收盘对应约 1.8 万亿美元，盘中一度突破 2 万亿美元。Anthropic 由此成为首家上市的前沿 AI 实验室（OpenAI 预计不早于 2027 年才上市）。

招股书披露：截至 2026 年 9 月 30 日的九个月收入 332.4 亿美元，同比增长约 1,200%；算力等长期支出承诺约 9,009 亿美元，延续至 2045 年。Anthropic 为特拉华州公益公司（public benefit corporation），最终招股书以书面形式告知 A 类股买方：公司可能把安全置于股东回报之上。

## 为什么值得被历史记住

AI 竞赛进入公开市场：17 天前（11 月 3 日），同一份 S-1/A 文件还在预警「递归自我改进可能很快达到」——如今预警者本身成了上市公司，其安全治理（公益公司章程、RSI 责任扩展政策、人类掌控继任模型的承诺）从自愿承诺走进持续的信息披露与股东监督。813 亿美元是资本市场对单一 AI 实验室最大的一笔下注，而招股书里 9,009 亿美元的算力承诺，说明这笔钱买的是未来二十年的基础设施。张力本身就是史实：上市首日纳斯达克跌 2.2%，ANT 却涨 17%——市场为故事定价，而非为当日定价；但「安全优先于回报」的章程能否顶住季度财报的压力仍未经检验，上市前后已有 AI 安全研究者发表「Misanthropic Statement」，质疑其安全承诺。

## 它带来的善

上市把一家以安全为使命的实验室放进阳光下：作为上市公司，它的治理、风险与安全承诺要接受持续披露、股东质询与公众审视；813 亿美元募资（另加 260 亿美元私募）为算力与安全研究提供了私人融资无法企及的规模，Claude 背后的安全工作有了更厚的资金底。保留提醒：公开市场的季度压力与「安全优先」章程之间的张力未经检验；首日涨幅是单日行情，不构成价值判断；招股书约 90 页的风险提示中包括「对人类构成灾难性或生存性风险」的警告。

# 2026-11-20 · Anthropic lists on Nasdaq in the largest IPO in history: the first frontier AI lab goes public

- **Date**: 2026-11-20
- **Category**: Policy (governance / listing)
- **Protagonist**: Anthropic

## What happened

Anthropic priced its initial public offering at $83.00 a share on November 18, 2026 (US time), above its $70–$78 indicated range, selling 980 million Class A shares — 956,351,354 by the company and 23,648,646 by selling stockholders — and raising $81.3 billion, the largest IPO in history by proceeds, passing SpaceX's $75 billion debut in June 2026. A separate $26 billion private placement was taken up at the same price. The shares began trading on the Nasdaq Global Select Market on November 19 under the ticker ANT (reported in Beijing on November 20): the first trade printed at $111.50, 34% above the offer price, and the stock closed at $97.17, up 17%, with about 840 million shares changing hands; the fully diluted valuation is about $1.5 trillion at the offer price and about $1.8 trillion at the close, briefly above $2 trillion intraday. Anthropic is the first frontier AI lab to list on a public exchange; OpenAI is not expected to pursue a listing before 2027.

The prospectus reports $33.24 billion of revenue for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, about 1,200% higher than a year earlier, and about $900.9 billion in spending commitments, largely for compute infrastructure, running through 2045. Anthropic is a Delaware public benefit corporation, and its final prospectus tells Class A buyers in writing that the company may choose safety over shareholder returns.

## Why it matters

The AI race enters the public markets: seventeen days after the same S-1/A filing warned that the Responsible Scaling Policy threshold for dramatically accelerated AI progress "may imminently" be met, the company that wrote the warning is itself a listed company, and its safety governance — the public-benefit charter, the Responsible Scaling Policy, the commitment to keep humans in control of successive frontier models — moves from voluntary pledges into continuous public disclosure and shareholder scrutiny. The $81.3 billion raise is the capital markets' biggest single bet on one AI lab, and the $900.9 billion in compute commitments it underwrites shows the infrastructure build-out behind it. The tension is the story now: the Nasdaq fell 2.2% on listing day while ANT rose 17% — the market priced the story, not the day — but whether a "safety before returns" charter can withstand quarterly earnings pressure is untested, and AI safety researchers issued a "Misanthropic Statement" around the listing challenging the company's safety claims.

## The good it brings

The listing puts a safety-mission lab in the daylight: as a public company its governance, risks and safety commitments face continuous disclosure, shareholder questions and public scrutiny, and the $81.3 billion raise — plus the $26 billion private placement — funds compute and safety research at a scale no private round could match, giving the safety work behind Claude a far thicker capital base. Caveats stay attached: the tension between quarterly market pressure and the safety-first charter is untested; a first-day gain is one day's trading, not a verdict on value; and the prospectus devotes about 90 pages to risk factors, including warnings of "catastrophic or existential risks to humanity."
