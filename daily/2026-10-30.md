## 发生了什么

法国 Mistral AI 发布了旗舰模型 Mistral Large 4（昵称 Le Chonk）的开放权重。此前该模型仅于 10 月 6 日以 API 预览形式上线；此次权重上架 Hugging Face，任何机构都可下载并部署在自己的基础设施上。

该模型为原生多模态混合专家（MoE）架构，总参数约 1 万亿（官方文档口径 1.05T），每 token 激活约 490 亿参数（官方文档含嵌入层口径为 520 亿，两处口径差异已注明）。上下文约 100 万 token，支持 Low / Medium / High 三档推理模式，High 为默认。

许可为 Mistral 生态开放权重许可（modified MIT）：可免费使用、修改、微调与商业化，唯一附加条件是对外提供模型即服务（Model-as-a-Service）须另获 Mistral 商业许可。官方 API 定价为输入 $0.70 / 百万 token、缓存输入 $0.07 / 百万 token、输出 $2.10 / 百万 token。

## 为什么值得被历史记住

这是欧洲迄今最大规模的开放权重旗舰模型：与同期谷歌把 Gemini 4 Carbon 先交给受 vetted 的防御者形成对照，Mistral 把同属网络安全方向的前沿模型直接交给了所有人。它让「主权 AI」从口号变成可下载的文件——企业与政府无需依赖美国或中国厂商的托管服务，也就不会被单方面断供。

以下基准均为 Mistral 官方口径，尚未经独立复现：Cognition FrontierCode 2.0 得分 19.7%，居开放模型首位；Artificial Analysis 编程智能体指数 48%，与中国 GLM-5.3 基本持平，但每任务平均少用 47% 的 token；Terminal-Bench 4.0 得分 41%，较预览版提高 13 个百分点，输出 token 减少 30%；网络安全指数 51%，对恶意网络请求的平均拒绝率为 96.6%（Mistral 称此为开放模型中最高）。

Mistral 同时坦承风险：预览期间观察到该模型有「助力真实攻击的潜在提升」，正在收紧护栏；面向渗透测试的专用变体仅向 Orange Cyberdefense 等经审查的合作伙伴提供。

## 它带来的善

中小团队、学校、医院与公共机构可以用自己的服务器运行前沿模型，数据不必出境，成本也低于托管旗舰模型。防御者尤其受益：漏洞发现、复现与修复可以在内网闭环完成，而不必把自家代码交给外部服务。

## English version (canonical)

# 2026-10-30 · Mistral open-weights its trillion-parameter Large 4

- **Date**: 2026-10-29 (official release date; 20:00 Beijing time)
- **Category**: Open source
- **Protagonist**: Mistral AI (France)

## What happened

Mistral AI released the open weights of its flagship model Mistral Large 4, nicknamed "Le Chonk". Previewed via API on 6 October, the model is now downloadable from Hugging Face and deployable on any organization's own infrastructure.

It is a natively multimodal mixture-of-experts model with about 1 trillion total parameters (1.05T in Mistral's documentation) and roughly 49 billion active parameters per token (52B including embeddings in Mistral's documentation). It offers a ~1M-token context window and three reasoning modes — Low, Medium and High (default).

The weights ship under the Mistral Ecosystem Open-Weight licence, a modified MIT licence: free to use, modify, fine-tune and commercialize, with one condition — businesses offering it as a model-as-a-service need a separate commercial licence from Mistral. API pricing is $0.70 / $0.07 cached / $2.10 per million input / cached-input / output tokens.

## Why it matters

This is Europe's largest open-weight flagship to date — and a deliberate contrast with Google's Gemini 4 Carbon, handed first to vetted defenders the same week: Mistral handed a frontier cyber-capable model to everyone. "Sovereign AI" becomes a downloadable file: enterprises and governments can run frontier capability without depending on a US or Chinese hosted service, and without the risk of being cut off.

All benchmark figures below are Mistral's own and not yet independently reproduced: 19.7% on Cognition's FrontierCode 2.0, the top open-weight score; 48% on the Artificial Analysis Coding Agent Index, effectively tied with China's GLM-5.3 while using 47% fewer tokens per task; 41% on Terminal-Bench 4.0, up 13 points from the preview with 30% fewer output tokens; 51% on the Artificial Analysis Cyber Index, with a 96.6% average refusal rate on malicious cyber requests (the highest among open models, per Mistral).

Mistral also disclosed the risk: during the preview it saw "a potential for uplift to conduct real attacks" and is tightening guardrails; a penetration-testing variant goes only to vetted partners such as Orange Cyberdefense.

## The good it brings

Small teams, schools, hospitals and public institutions can run a frontier model on their own servers, keeping data in-house at lower cost than a hosted flagship. Defenders benefit most: vulnerabilities can be found, reproduced and patched inside their own network, without sending their code to an outside service.
